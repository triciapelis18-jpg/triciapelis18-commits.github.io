About Mobile Legends

What is Mobile Legends?

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a popular 5v5 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed by MOONTON. Players compete in teams using unique heroes with different skills and roles. The objective is to destroy the enemy's base while working together through strategy, teamwork, and quick decision-making.
