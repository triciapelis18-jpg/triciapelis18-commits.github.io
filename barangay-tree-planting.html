🌳 Barangay Tree-Planting Projects

On November 10, 2024 – 7:00 AM at Kias, Baguio City, the Sangguniang Kabataan of Kias partnered with the Youth For Peace Baguio Chapter for the activity titled GREEN LEGACY: URBAN AFFORESTATION with the theme, “SDG 13: Building a Greener Kias – The Youth’s Initiative to Cultivate Tomorrow Through Tree Planting and Bi-Yearly Monitoring.”

I helped plant trees and clear areas as part of our barangay’s effort to bring back our forests and protect the environment. This activity taught me the value of taking care of our natural resources and preventing disasters like landslides and floods. It also made me realize that small actions from many people can make a big difference in keeping our community safe and green.

Skills and Values Gained

  • Environmental awareness
  • Teamwork and cooperation
  • Community service
  • Discipline and responsibility
  • Leadership through volunteerism
